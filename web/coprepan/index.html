CO.PRE.PAN

Corpus de Prensa Panhispánica

Research corpus project at Marburg University.

CO.PRE.PAN is a research infrastructure for the systematic acquisition, preservation and corpus-linguistic analysis of Spanish-language digital press material from the Spanish-speaking world.

The project is currently under development.
